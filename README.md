# The Axiom Continuum

**A universe of discovery.**

The Axiom Continuum is an evolving creative and educational universe designed to inspire curiosity about mathematics and science through imagination, storytelling, and exploration.

> To inspire lifelong curiosity in mathematics and science through imagination and discovery, empowering learners to explore, understand, and create.

## Website

**Official domain:** [theaxiomcontinuum.com](https://theaxiomcontinuum.com)

This repository contains the **public-facing website** for The Axiom Continuum. The site is hosted using **GitHub Pages**, with the domain managed separately through GoDaddy.

The current release is an introductory landing page, not the finished learning experience. The custom domain has been configured; HTTPS certificate activation may still be in progress.

## Where We're Going

Our initial creative and educational experience will introduce **Decara**, the first World of The Axiom Continuum, and **VECTOR TEN — The Adventures of The Decad**, a cast of ten sentient feline mathematical characters associated with the digits 0–9.

Early website development is planned as a **section-first, horizontal prototype**: begin with foundational Counting experiences across the ten digit Sectors, evaluate what works, and refine the experience before expanding. Planned features and stories are not yet live.

We aim to make learning imaginative and welcoming while maintaining mathematical accuracy, appropriate learner safeguards, and meaningful educational accessibility.
